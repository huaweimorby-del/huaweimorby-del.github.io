See pricing How it works
Flipmetry finds potentially underpriced listings faster. It compares asking prices, not confirmed sale prices, and does not guarantee profit, a sale, or a specific sell-through rate. You're still the one who checks condition, photos, listing accuracy, and seller legitimacy — Flipmetry's job is to shorten how long that takes to get right.
How It Works

Not a scraper. A pricing memory.

Most tools show you a listing. Flipmetry remembers every listing it's ever seen, and compares a new one against real, genuinely comparable history — not a guess.

◎

Historical listing tracking

Every listing gets a stable identity and a full, immutable history — price changes and availability over time, not a one-off snapshot.

◇

Real product matching

Listings are matched to the specific comparable product they actually are — "iPhone 14 Pro," not just "phone" — independent of whichever search found them.

▤

Observed asking-price market

A price baseline per product built from the asking prices Flipmetry has seen: median, percentiles and sample size, shown only once there's enough comparable data. These are asking prices, not what items finally sell for. "Insufficient data" is reported honestly, never guessed.

↻

Flipmetry Calibration

A dedicated warm-up mode that deliberately sweeps a marketplace's category tree before normal scanning starts surfacing opportunities — so there is more observed pricing to compare against sooner.

✓

Buy/sell pipeline

Found → Saved → Bought → Listed → Sold, with the amounts you enter, so realised profit comes from what you actually paid and received, not an estimate.

⌂

Runs on your machine

No cloud backend, no accounts beyond your licence. Flipmetry runs against your own logged-in marketplace sessions on your own PC, so it scans while Flipmetry is open and your computer is on. Your data and logins never leave your device.
