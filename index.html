How It Works

Not a scraper. A pricing memory.

Most tools show you a listing. Flipmetry remembers every listing it's ever seen, and compares a new one against real, genuinely comparable history — not a guess.

◎

Historical listing tracking

Every listing gets a stable identity and a full, immutable history — price changes and availability over time, not a one-off snapshot.

◇

Real product matching

Listings are matched to the specific comparable product they actually are — "iPhone 14 Pro," not just "phone" — independent of whichever search found them.

▤

Observed market pricing

A live, identity-aware price baseline per product — median, percentiles, sample size — shown only once there's real comparable data. "Insufficient data" is reported honestly, never guessed.

↻

Flipmetry Calibration

A dedicated warm-up mode that deliberately sweeps a marketplace's category tree before normal scanning starts surfacing opportunities — so pricing has something real to compare against from day one.

✓

Buy/sell pipeline

Found → Saved → Bought → Listed → Sold, with real invested/sold amounts, so realised profit comes from what you actually paid and received — never an estimate.

⌂

Runs on your machine

No cloud backend, no accounts beyond your licence. Flipmetry runs against your own logged-in marketplace sessions — your data and credentials never leave your device.
