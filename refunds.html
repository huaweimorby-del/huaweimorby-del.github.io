Refund & Subscription Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026
Working draft. This document has not yet been reviewed by a solicitor and will be updated as that review completes.

Subscription billing

Flipmetry subscriptions renew automatically each month, on the plan you selected at checkout, until cancelled. Billing is handled entirely by our payment provider — you can view and manage your subscription, payment method, and invoices at any time via the Customer Portal linked from Settings → Licence in the app.

Cancelling

You may cancel at any time via the Customer Portal. If you cancel, you retain full access through the end of your current paid billing period — we do not cut off access immediately while you still have paid time remaining. After that period ends, Flipmetry enters read-only mode: your existing data stays fully visible and exportable, but new marketplace monitoring pauses until you reactivate.

Refunds

We consider refund requests on a case-by-case basis. Contact us at the email below with your order details and the reason for your request, and we'll respond promptly.

Price changes

Founder-tier pricing (Beta / Super Founder / Founder) is intended to remain locked for as long as a subscriber's subscription continues uninterrupted, as a deliberate early-adopter incentive. Standard-tier pricing may change with reasonable advance notice to existing subscribers.

Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, our payment provider will typically retry the charge automatically over the following days before the subscription lapses. Once a subscription lapses, Flipmetry enters read-only mode as described above.

Contact

Billing questions can be sent to support@flipmetry.app.
