Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026
Working draft. This document is written to accurately reflect what the current Flipmetry application actually does, not a generic template. It has not yet been reviewed by a solicitor and will be updated as that review completes.

The short version

Flipmetry is local-first. Your marketplace scanning activity, listing history, and pricing data are stored only on your own computer. We do not have a copy of them, and normal use of the product does not send them to us. The only things that leave your device are what's strictly needed to (1) validate your software licence and (2) check for app updates — both described below.

What stays on your device (not sent to us)

  • Marketplace listing history, observations, and pricing baselines
  • Your search targets and watched categories
  • Your buy/sell pipeline records (Found → Saved → Bought → Listed → Sold, and the amounts involved)
  • Your logged-in marketplace browser sessions (Vinted/Facebook) — these live in a local, persistent browser profile on your machine and are never transmitted to Flipmetry infrastructure
  • Application settings and calibration state

We do not run background telemetry or analytics. There is no silent or automatic usage-data upload anywhere in the product.

What we can and cannot see

We do not operate a server that receives your marketplace data. Our payment/licensing provider processes your licence key and device ID under their own privacy policy, which we recommend reviewing alongside this one.

Diagnostic exports

If you use Settings → Export Diagnostics, Flipmetry builds a bundle for you to review and send to support manually. That bundle excludes your licence key, cookies, tokens, browser profile contents, and passwords. Sending it to us is entirely your choice; we never receive it automatically.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you have rights under UK/EU data protection law, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the limited licensing data (licence key / device ID) held on your behalf by our payment provider. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page.
